About University of Dukes

A Legacy of Excellence, A Future of Innovation

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized Center of Excellence in Scholarship, Research, and Training, empowering lifelong learning and fostering innovation for the betterment of humanity.

Our Mission

To provide transformative education that develops critical thinkers, ethical leaders, and socially responsible global citizens who contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Rich History

Founded in 1965, University of Dukes has grown from a small liberal arts college to a world-renowned research institution. Over six decades, we've remained committed to our founding principle: education that transforms lives and shapes society.

Today, we stand as a beacon of academic excellence, with over 15,000 students from 100+ countries, 2,000 distinguished faculty members, and a legacy of groundbreaking discoveries that have changed the world.
