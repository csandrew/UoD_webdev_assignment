Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for admission?
You can apply online through our Admissions page. The application deadline for Fall semester is July 15th. Required documents include transcripts, recommendation letters, and a personal statement.
What financial aid options are available?
We offer merit-based scholarships, need-based grants, work-study programs, and federal student loans. Over 80% of our students receive some form of financial aid.
Is on-campus housing guaranteed?
On-campus housing is guaranteed for all first-year students. Upperclassmen can apply for housing through a lottery system. We also offer off-campus housing assistance.
Do you accept international students?
Yes! We have students from over 100 countries. We offer English language programs, international student orientation, and dedicated support services.
What is the average class size?
The average class size is 22 students, with a student-to-faculty ratio of 12:1. This ensures personalized attention and meaningful interaction with professors.
How can I schedule a campus tour?
Campus tours are available Monday through Friday at 10 AM and 2 PM. You can schedule a tour by calling our Admissions Office or booking online through our website.
